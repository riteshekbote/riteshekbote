# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

I take security research and responsible disclosure seriously.

If you believe you have found a security issue in any of my public repositories, **do not open a public issue**. Instead, report it privately.

- **Email:** ritesh@ekbote.dev
- **Subject:** `[SECURITY] <repository-name>: <short description>`

Please include:

- The affected repository and file/commit
- A description of the issue and its impact
- Steps to reproduce (if applicable)
- A suggested fix (optional)

### What to expect

I aim to acknowledge receipt within 3 business days and triage the report within 10 business days. I will not share your report without your permission.

## Scope

This policy applies to the code I author and maintain. For bugs in upstream projects (forks), please report to the original repository's maintainers.
